Route Replay: questions and answers

Route Replay draws a trip on a 3D Earth: each flight, train, bus, drive and
ferry in the sunlight of its own day and hour. Credits for the imagery, data
and software are at {{ORIGIN}}/acknowledgments.


THE CHAT

What happens to the itinerary I type into the chat?
  To read it, Route Replay sends the text to Cloudflare Workers AI, on the
  same network that hosts this site. Neither Cloudflare nor Route Replay
  keeps the text or uses it to train models: the model reads it, Route
  Replay turns what it found into a journey, and the text is discarded.
  Each new message sends the conversation so far, to be read the same way.

  Your browser keeps each draft's conversation, so it's there when you open
  the draft again in the same browser. It isn't sent anywhere else.

What does a draft keep?
  For each leg: its airports, its local departure and arrival times, and its
  aircraft or how it was travelled. Also a title made from the place names.
  Not your messages.

Why does the chat ask for local times?
  The Sun, Moon, stars and clouds are drawn for the moment of each leg, so
  local times put day and night in the right place. The chat can't look up
  schedules, so when you give only a flight number it asks for the times,
  and your own AI can look them up (see below).

Why is an aircraft "typical"?
  When you don't name the aircraft, the chat picks a typical one for the
  distance: a narrow-body jet for shorter flights and a wide-body for long
  ones. Name the type and it changes.

What can my own AI do?
  When the chat needs something that can be looked up, such as a flight's
  times or a ferry timetable, it offers a request to copy into ChatGPT,
  Claude, Gemini or another AI. Paste the AI's answer back into the chat.

  An AI agent that can make web requests can also build the whole journey
  itself: copy the prompt from the chat's first message into it, or point it
  at {{ORIGIN}}/agent-docs.

How many messages can I send?
  A limited number each hour, which the chat shows, and there's a daily
  limit for everyone. When they run out, your own AI agent can make the
  journey instead.


DRAFTS AND SHARING

Who can see my draft?
  Only someone with one of its links. A draft the chat makes stays open in
  your browser for {{SESSION_DAYS}} days. Its private link opens it anywhere,
  and anyone with that link can edit, publish or delete the draft, so keep it
  to yourself. Route Replay stores only a fingerprint of the link's key, not
  the key itself, so it can't send you the link again.

How long are drafts kept?
  A draft that nobody has changed for {{DRAFT_RETENTION_DAYS}} days is deleted, unless it
  was published.

What does publishing do?
  It makes a watch link that anyone with the link can play. Watch pages are
  kept out of search engines, but chat apps show a preview with the
  journey's title and places when you paste the link. A published journey
  stays until its draft is deleted.

Can I download the video?
  Yes: Export makes a video file in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.


THE GLOBE

Are the flight paths real?
  They're illustrations, not recorded tracks. Each flight follows the great
  circle, the shortest path between its airports.

Where do trains, buses, drives and ferries go?
  Along railways, roads and ferry lines from OpenStreetMap, looked up through
  BRouter and OSRM when the journey is saved. Where no route is found, the
  leg follows the great circle.

Where do the clouds come from?
  From NOAA's Global Forecast System weather model for the journey's times:
  its analysis, or its forecast for times after the journey was saved. They
  are not satellite photos.
