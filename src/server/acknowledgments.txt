EARTH IMAGERY

Blue Marble: Next Generation, monthly base map (2004)
  Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon, NASA Earth Observatory
  https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/

Black Marble 2016 (city lights at night)
  NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Suomi NPP VIIRS
  data from Miguel Román, NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BlackMarble20161km.jpg

Both images are in the public domain. Route Replay is not affiliated with
or endorsed by NASA.

ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model (mountains and valleys,
shaded by the Sun and Moon)
  NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, 2022. Public domain.
  https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74

AIRCRAFT

The aircraft are original models built in code from published dimensions
in the manufacturers' airport planning documents (Airbus, ATR, Boeing,
De Havilland Canada, Embraer, and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries for the CRJ).
Route Replay is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.


AIRPORT DATA

OurAirports (public domain): airport codes, names and coordinates
  https://ourairports.com/data/

mwgg/Airports (MIT License): airport time zones
  Copyright (c) 2014 mwgg
  https://github.com/mwgg/Airports

@photostructure/tz-lookup (CC0 1.0): time zones for the few airports

  https://github.com/photostructure/tz-lookup


WEATHER

NOAA Global Forecast System (GFS): cloud water and 700 hPa wind, from the
NOAA Open Data Dissemination Program on AWS. Public domain.
  https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-gfs-bdp-pds/


COASTLINES

Natural Earth (public domain): land, island, ice shelf, and lake outlines
that mark open water for sun glint and sky reflection. Made with Natural Earth.
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/


GROUND ROUTES

Train, bus, car, and ferry legs follow railways, roads, and ferry lines
from OpenStreetMap. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available
under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Routes are looked up when a journey is saved, through two community-run
services, with thanks:
  BRouter (railways), by Arndt Brenschede: https://brouter.de/
  OSRM (roads and ferries), hosted by FOSSGIS e.V.:
  https://routing.openstreetmap.de/


SUN AND MOON

Sun and Moon positions use the low-precision formulas from The
Astronomical Almanac, published by the U.S. Naval Observatory and
HM Nautical Almanac Office.
